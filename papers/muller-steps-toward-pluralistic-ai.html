Steps toward Pluralistic AI

Michael Muller · Aminat Adebiyi · Katelyn Morrison — Independent Researcher, Medford, MA, USA; IBM Thomas J. Watson Research Center, Yorktown Heights, NY, USA; Microsoft, Redmond, WA, USA

Download PDF All accepted papers
Abstract

Position paper

In this position paper, we sketch a series of formal and informal studies of AI agents, leading toward implementations of Pluralistic AI. The goal is to create and refine approaches that position the AI in the role of a non-human advisor to one or more human participants. The advice may take diverse structures, including (a) generation of directed, diverse outcome contents; (b) generation of directed, diverse outcome presentation-styles; (c) generation of contents based on one or more selected perspectives; and (d) generation of contents that are required to provide labeled, divergent perspectives (Pluralistic AI). This work seeks to find alternatives to “oracle” AI agents that are limited to content from a single totalizing culture, and to encourage humans to take responsibility for interpretations, decisions, and actions following advice or provocations from the AI agent.

Keywords: Human centered AI (HCAI), Human Computer Interaction (HCI), Generative AI, Diversity, Pluralism, Cultures
